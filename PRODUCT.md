# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Women in Rabat (and visitors) looking for premium beauty services: Russian manicure, Brazilian blowout, lash extensions, facials, makeup. French-speaking, mostly arriving from Instagram on their phones. They are comparison-shopping salons; the site's job is to make the quality of the work feel self-evident and to convert them into a booking (online form, phone call, or Instagram DM).

A secondary audience is the salon staff who use the /dashboard back-office; that surface is product register and out of scope for this file.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site + online booking for Brazilian Studio by Ali K, a real salon at 21 rue Oued Sbou, Rabat-Agdal. Success = booking requests. Every public page should shorten the distance between "this place looks exceptional" and "réserver".

## Brand Personality

Polished, warm, precise. The salon's signature is Russian-technique precision inside a plush, flower-arched, pink interior (see og-image.jpg: neon script logo, cherry-blossom arches, cream sofas). The site should feel like the salon: light, airy, meticulously groomed. Luxury through calm and detail, not through darkness or drama.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing-page tropes (gradient text, glassmorphism cards, hero-metric stats grids).
- Flashy template effects: 3D card flips, shimmer sweeps, bouncing arrows. They read as cheap; this brand's confirmed motion direction is calm and smooth.
- Dark "luxury spa" clichés (black + gold serif). The salon's physical identity is light pink; the site stays light.
- Beauty-clinic sterility: pure white, teal accents, stocky sans headings.

## Design Principles

1. **The salon is the brand.** Photography of real work and the real interior carries the page; UI recedes.
2. **Precision is the product.** Alignment, spacing, and typographic rhythm must be as groomed as a Russian manicure. Sloppy details contradict the core promise.
3. **Calm motion, exponential ease.** One orchestrated reveal per section, ease-out curves, nothing bounces or spins. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
4. **Every path ends at Réserver.** No dead links; each section offers a next step toward booking, phone, or Instagram.
5. **Identity-preservation.** Pink/rose-gold palette and Playfair Display + Inter are shipped brand decisions; refine their use, don't replace them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on all text (watch pink-on-cream). Visible keyboard focus on all interactive elements. 44px touch targets (mobile-heavy audience). `prefers-reduced-motion` honored globally. French-language content with `lang="fr"`.
